About Youth Business Association

Building access, confidence, and real-world business readiness.

YBA exists to help students move from curiosity to capability through mentorship, practical learning, and community.

Who We Are

A nonprofit designed to connect students with opportunity.

We bridge the space between classroom learning and the professional world so students can explore business in a way that feels real, relevant, and empowering.

Our programs are intentionally practical. Students meet professionals, ask honest questions, build foundational knowledge, and practice the kinds of communication and problem-solving skills that matter in college, careers, and entrepreneurship. Explore our student programs or see the impact we've made so far.

Youth Business Association is a student-centered nonprofit focused on making business education more accessible, engaging, and useful for young people who want to lead, create, and make an impact.
