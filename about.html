About Youth Business Association

Who Are We?

YBA is a student-focused business education program that makes business easier to understand — through free workshops, panels, events, and community.

Business shouldn't feel like something only adults in suits understand. YBA helps students explore marketing, finance, leadership, entrepreneurship, and other areas of business in a way that feels approachable and real.

Our opportunities are designed to be free and accessible without financial barriers. Students can hear from people with business experience, practice real skills, work with other students, and figure out where they fit within business.
