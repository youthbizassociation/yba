What is YBA?

We help students turn curiosity into action.

YBA is a student-focused nonprofit built to make business education accessible, practical, and community-driven. We connect students with the tools, people, and experiences they need to explore careers, projects, and leadership in a realistic way.

01

Accessible learning

Open, free opportunities for students to grow without financial barriers.

02

Real-world exposure

Workshops, panels, and community events designed to make business feel relevant.

03

Student leadership

A community that values initiative, collaboration, and long-term growth.
