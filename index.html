What is YBA?

Business shouldn't feel like something only adults in suits understand.

YBA is a student-focused business education program that creates free online and in-person opportunities for students to learn about business — without financial barriers. We help students go from "I don't know where to start" to "I understand how business works, I've practiced these skills, and I know what I can do next."
