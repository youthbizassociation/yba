Questions students and families often ask.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is YBA?

YBA is a student-focused business education program. We create free online and in-person opportunities for students to learn about business through workshops, panels, events, and community — all without financial barriers.

Who can join YBA?

YBA is for students who want to learn about business, entrepreneurship, leadership, and related topics. You don't need any prior experience — we're beginner-friendly.

Does YBA cost money?

No. YBA's opportunities are designed to be free and accessible without financial barriers. Everything is free to join and participate in. Learn more on our Join page.

What kinds of opportunities does YBA offer?

YBA offers workshops on topics like marketing, finance, and leadership. We host speaker panels, networking events, and project-based activities. Students can also get involved through our Discord community and social media.

Do I need previous business experience?

No. YBA is designed to help students who are completely new to business learn in a comfortable, supportive environment. You can also participate if you already have experience and want to build on it.

Can I join if I don't know what area of business interests me?

Absolutely. One of the best things about YBA is that you can explore different areas — marketing, finance, leadership, entrepreneurship, and more — before deciding what fits you best.

Are YBA opportunities online or in person?

Both. YBA offers online and in-person opportunities so students can participate no matter where they are.

How can I participate in YBA events?

Check our Events page for upcoming opportunities. You can also join our Discord to get notified about new events as they're announced.

Where can I stay updated?

Follow us on Instagram, join our Discord, or check the Events page for the latest updates.

How can I contact YBA?

Reach us at youthbusinessassociation@outlook.com or connect with us through our Discord or Instagram.
