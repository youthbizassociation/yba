What it is used for

Announcements, updates, and community building.

The YBA Discord is designed to be a quick, welcoming place for members to stay in the loop and connect with each other outside of formal events.

  • Event announcements and reminders for upcoming workshops, panels, and student activities.
  • Social media notifications and quick updates about YBA highlights and opportunities.
  • Important organizational updates, resources, and deadlines for members.
  • Automated reminders and community check-ins to keep members informed.
